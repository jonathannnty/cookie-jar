Cookie Jar

Tailor your cookies!

Cookies are small bits of data stored in your browser that help websites remember things like your login, preferences, and settings to create a smoother browsing experience.

But not all cookies are harmless — some are used to track your activity across websites, collect personal data, and build detailed profiles for advertising and analytics.

With Cookie Jar, you can manage your cookie preferences at a glance, and set your default preferences so you don't have to choose between convenience and privacy.

Session Cookies 👍

Session cookies are temporary cookies that help websites remember you while you use them — like keeping you logged in or saving your cart. They disappear once you close your browser.

Authentication Cookies 👍

Authentication cookies help websites know that you're logged in. They store a secure ID so you can stay signed in while browsing without entering your password on every page.

Tracking Cookies 👎

Tracking cookies follow your activity across websites to collect data about your behavior, interests, and browsing habits — often to personalize ads and analytics.

Third-Party Cookies 👎

Third-party cookies are set by websites other than the one you're currently visiting. They're commonly used for cross-site tracking, targeted advertising, and embedded content like social media buttons.
