Cookie Jar

Manage your cookie preferences at a glance — and set defaults so you never have to choose between convenience and privacy.

Why should you care about cookies?

Cookies are bits of information stored in your browser that help tailor your experience — but some cookies take it a step too far, using that information to target ads and build detailed profiles of your online activity.

93.7 billion stolen tracking cookies were found on the dark web in 2025 — linked to major platforms like Google and YouTube — allowing attackers to log into accounts without passwords.

With Cookie Jar, you can manage your preferences at a glance. Set defaults once and the extension handles the rest automatically on every site you visit.
